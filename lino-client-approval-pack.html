Lino Barbers Website Direction

Three separate barber brands. One simple website system.

The plan is to build Il Barbiere, Histon Barbers and Parker’s Barbers as separate-looking websites, while controlling them from one shared GitHub/Vercel codebase. Booksy remains the booking system.

Proposed look and feel

Il Barbiere

Classic Cambridge heritage.

Premium, established, understated. Serif type, black/ivory, muted gold. Avoids trendy/hipster styling.

Histon Barbers

Local, friendly, reliable.

Village/community feel. Warm neutral palette. Easy mobile booking. Strong for Histon, Impington and nearby customers.

Parker’s Barbers

Sharp, clean, modern Cambridge.

A cleaner contemporary identity so Parker’s does not feel like a copy of Il Barbiere or Histon.

What the websites include

Each siteHome, services, gallery/photos, find us, contact, Booksy booking button.
BookingEvery shop gets its own Booksy link/widget. Customers should always reach the correct shop booking page.
ManagementOne shared template/codebase. Shop details changed from one simple config file.
HostingGitHub + Vercel. Fast, low-cost, easy to update.
Mobile-firstDesigned mainly for phone users who want to find the shop and book quickly.

What I need confirmed

For each shop: final trading name, address, phone number, opening hours, correct Booksy link/widget, Instagram/Facebook, whether prices should be shown on the site, and any real shop/team photos.

Until these are confirmed, the starter build will use placeholders and the known Histon Booksy link.

Decision needed first: choose whether the three directions above feel right: Il Barbiere = heritage, Histon = local/village, Parker’s = modern/sharp.
